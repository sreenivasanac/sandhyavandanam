1. Aachamanam (External cleansing):

Procedure: For “Om achyutaaya … govindaaya namah |” Pour a small amount
of water in your right palm and swallow without sipping for every mantram. For
“Keshava … daamodara” perform these in order: (keshava) touch right cheek
with right thumb, (naaraayana) touch left cheek with right thumb, (maadhava)
touch right eye with right ring finger, (govinda) touch left eye with right ring
finger, (vishno) touch right side of nose with right index finger,
(madhusuudana) touch left side of nose with right index finger, (trivikrama)
touch right ear with right little finger, (vaamana) touch left ear with right little
finger, (shriidhara) touch right shoulder with right middle finger, (hriishikesha)
touch left shoulder with right middle finger, (padmanaabha) touch navel with
all right fingers, (daamodara) touch top of the head with all right fingers.

ओं अच्युताय नमः; ओं अनन्ताय नमः। ओं गोविन्दाय नमः।
केशव, नारायण, माधव, गोविन्द, विष्णो, मपुसूदन, त्रिविक्रम, वामन,
श्रीर, हृषीकेश, पदमनाभ, दामोदर।
ஓம்‌ அச்யுதாய நம:| ஓம்‌ அநந்தாய நம:।| ஓம்‌ கோவிந்தாய நம:|
கேஷவ, நாராயண, மாதவ, கோவிந்த, விஷ்ணொ, மதுஸதந,
த்ரிவிக்ரம, வாமன, ஸ்ரீதர, ஹ்ருஷீகெஷ, பத்மநாப, தாமோதர]

Procedure: Close your right palm on your left palm and place it on your right
thigh while reciting. Then write on the water “Om Kesavaaya Namah” with ring
finger and put tilakam on your forehead with the water.

5. Prokshanam (External purification):

Procedure: For “Om sri keshavaaya … arangamaama vah |” sprinkle water on
your head with your ring finger. For “Yasya kshayaaya jinvatha |” sprinkle on
both the feet by reciting. For “Aapo janayathaa chanah |” sprinkle water on
your head with your ring finger. For “Om bhuurbhuvassuvah |” sprinkle some
water around your head after reciting.
